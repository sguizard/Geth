Single-cell RNA-seq of peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) from four Wistar rats.
10x Genomics GEM-X Universal 3' Gene Expression v4 with On-Chip Multiplexing (OCM): the cells of the four rats
were loaded in one chip channel, each rat with its own OCM barcode, so all four rats are in one library,
sequenced on six lanes. Barcodes: rat_1 OB1 (female), rat_2 OB2 (female), rat_3 OB3 (male), rat_4 OB4 (male).
About 2,500 cells per rat. This is a small test: reads are subsampled and the reference is chromosome 6 only.

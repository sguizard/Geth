ChIP-seq of the transcription elongation factor Spt5 in budding yeast, at two time points, 0 min and 15 min.
Two independent cultures (biological replicates 1 and 2) per time point.
- CHIP01: Spt5, 0 min, culture 1.  CHIP02: Spt5, 0 min, culture 2.
- CHIP03: Spt5, 15 min, culture 1. CHIP04: Spt5, 15 min, culture 2.
- INPUT01: input DNA (no antibody) of culture 1, the control for CHIP01 and CHIP03.
- INPUT02: input DNA of culture 2, the control for CHIP02 and CHIP04.
Illumina paired-end, 76 bp reads. Reference: S. cerevisiae R64 (Ensembl chromosome names).
